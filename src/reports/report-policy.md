# Report Policy

The report writer must follow these rules:

1. The PDF guide is the first rule.
2. Write from the specific account owner's point of view.
3. Do not write administrator-style text such as "I controlled five accounts".
4. Only write verified completed actions as completed.
5. Failed or skipped actions must be written as problems, adjustments, or follow-up tasks.
6. Keep body text black.
7. Preserve the five master reports:
